BUILT TO BE INSPECTED

A real service. An honest boundary.

The assistant runs combined CPU inference. Disaggregated workers, KV-transfer experiments, and GPU estimates remain in the separate operator lab.

Evidence, not decoration

TTFT includes retrieval and model waiting after authentication. Usage comes from the model server. Unknown reasoning or cache counts stay unknown.

Small by design

One concurrent answer, short contexts, daily quotas, a monthly token ceiling, and a 90-second deadline.

Privacy with limits

Keys are hashed. Content is transient. Syntactic citations do not guarantee accuracy; verify sources before relying on an answer.
